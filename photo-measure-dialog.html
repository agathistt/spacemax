Photo-assisted measurements

A photo. A reference.
A starting estimate.

Use a straight-on photo, one known length, and a few marks to estimate width and height for your planner.

Using these estimates will replace the planner’s current width and height. Depth stays manual.

JPEG, PNG, or WebP · Up to 15 MB · Your photo stays in this browser and is cleared when you close this window.

Use a straight-on photo. The reference and the target must be in the same flat plane. Keep the target’s edges horizontal and vertical in the image. Angled views and objects at different depths will give unreliable estimates.

Click or tap the image to place the selected point. With the image focused, use arrow keys to move the crosshair, Shift + arrow for fine movement, and Enter or Space to place it. You can also enter point coordinates below.
